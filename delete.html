🗑️ Account Deletion Request

App Name: ChiChi Buzz
Developer: ChiChi Buzz Team

Important: Our app does not allow users to create accounts or store personal data directly. However, if you have submitted content (like messages or feedback) that you wish to have removed, please follow the steps below.

📋 Steps to Request Deletion

  1. Open the ChiChi Buzz app on your device.
  2. Go to the "Support" or "Contact Us" section within the app.
  3. Send a deletion request with the following details:
    • Your device ID (found in app settings)
    • Your email address for confirmation
    • Specific content or data you want deleted
  4. Alternatively, email us directly at: support@chichi.buzz with the subject line "Account Data Deletion Request".
  5. We will process your request and confirm via email within 7 business days.

📌 What Happens to Your Data

Data deleted: All messages, feedback, and metadata associated with your device ID will be permanently removed from our active databases.

Data retained: We may retain anonymized analytics (non-personal) for internal reporting. No personal identifiable information is stored.

Retention period: After deletion, backups are cleared within 30 days.

🔒 Privacy Commitment: ChiChi Buzz does not collect or store personal data without your explicit consent. This page complies with Google Play's Data Safety policy.
